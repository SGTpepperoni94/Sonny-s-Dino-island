1. Core Vision, Family Cast & Full-Game Scope
* Working Title & Family Cast: A sunlit, joyous 3D pirate & dinosaur open-world discovery game starring Sonny (age 3, playable captain) and his baby brother Artie (pirate sidekick/co-captain). Captain Seahawk (Dad) is the friendly harbor mentor chatting in Animalese with picture speech bubbles. Pirate Pete is the goofy, harmless LEGO-style villain who pops into coins and confetti when bonked. Includes a special Pirate Bed Ship home vessel easter egg.
* Zero-Reading Design (100% Language-Free): Playable without reading English. All guidance uses picture speech bubbles, universal icons, glowing cues, expressive animations, and Animal Crossing-style "Animalese" gibberish synth voices.
* One Expansive 6-Biome Island (7/7 UI-Free Reference Images Locked):
   * Pirate Harbor & Sunny Meadow / Future Dino Ranch (South Coast — Spawn): Wide golden-sand bay curving up into a flat lime-green meadow plateau, with a worn dirt trail inland, Pirate Kit shacks/barrels/palms, and Ship_Small anchored offshore.
   * Whispering Fern Jungle (West): Rolling emerald-green hills with a winding warm ochre dirt footpath and lush foliage clusters.
   * Ancient Dino Ruins (Northwest Ridge): Elevated stepped mossy-stone plateaus connected by natural walkable sloped ramps overlooking the ocean.
   * Smoking Volcano Ridge (Northeast Peak): Charcoal-purple faceted volcano with a safe, wide walkable sandy-rock ridge trail, a glowing warm amber-orange lava channel set cleanly below the path, and rising stylized low-poly white smoke puffs.
   * Crystal Waterfall Lagoon (East Coast): Sandy aquamarine cove fed by an animated white-and-cyan waterfall cascading down a mossy cliff with foamy splash rings.
* Skull Rock Pirate Cave (Southeast Coastline — Between Pirate Harbor's East Edge and Crystal Waterfall Lagoon): Carved into the coastal cliff headland facing the turquoise bay so Sonny spots it from the harbor. Features a massive, kid-friendly low-poly carved pirate skull wearing a red bandana and eyepatch with a warm glowing amber eye socket, plus an open-jaw archway leading into a torchlit interior cavern with BOTH a walkable stone ledge (connected to the beach trail) and a shallow turquoise water inlet channel (for Build 2+ rafting).
* Full-Game Features (Build 2+ — DO NOT BUILD IN BUILD 1, ARCHITECT HOOKS ONLY):
   * Dino Eggs & Full Quaternius Dino Roster: Color-coded, easy-to-find Dino Eggs (many out in the open + gentle sparkle/chime cues). Pick up and throw to hatch every dino in the Quaternius Animated Dinosaurs pack. Hatched dinos follow Sonny, live in the Dino Ranch, fill a Picture Dino-Dex, and when ridden have both a playful traversal ability AND a specific cartoon attack.
   * Zero-Cost Hybrid Building: Free glowing LEGO-Style Build Pads across the island (hold 1 button to snap bridges, ziplines, campfires, pens) + Free Sticker-Stamp Builder inside the Dino Ranch. Coins and gems are pure celebratory shinies.
   * Multi-Modal Traversal & Toys: Rideable dinos, ziplines, coastal wooden raft, simple 1-button fishing (Prop_Fish_Tuna, Prop_Fish_Mackerel, Prop_Bucket_Fishes), pull-levers, movable objects, smashable barrels, and goofy LEGO-style combat (zero puzzles, zero gore, zero punishing game overs).

3.1 Proven Stack, Renderer & Lighting (Reuse Exactly)
* Packages: three (^0.187.0), react / react-dom (^19), vite, tailwindcss, typescript ("strict": true).
* Renderer & Software-WebGL Guard: Detect SwiftShader|llvmpipe|softpipe|Software|Basic Render via UNMASKED_RENDERER_WEBGL. If software WebGL: disable AA/shadows, pixelRatio = 1, skip renderer.compile(). On hardware WebGL: antialias: true, powerPreference: 'high-performance', outputColorSpace = THREE.SRGBColorSpace, toneMapping = THREE.ACESFilmicToneMapping, toneMappingExposure = 0.98, pixelRatio = Math.min(window.devicePixelRatio, isTouch ? 1.5 : 2), shadowMap.enabled = true, shadowMap.type = THREE.PCFSoftShadowMap. Call renderer.compile(scene, camera) once post-generation.
* Sky, Fog, PMREM & Moving Shadow Frustum:
   * scene.background = new THREE.Color(0x1d9bf0), scene.fog = new THREE.FogExp2(0x5cc3ff, 0.00075).
   * PMREMGenerator from RoomEnvironment (0.04) -> scene.environment, dispose pmrem, retain and dispose envRenderTarget on teardown.
   * HemisphereLight(0x6ecaff, 0xd99b4e, 0.88) + DirectionalLight(0xfff3d1, 1.5) offset (-45, 72, -35) from Sonny.
   * Move dirLight.position and dirLight.target.position every frame to track Sonny with a compact 1024x1024 ortho frustum (±55, near: 5, far: 220, bias: -0.0005, normalBias: 0.02).
* 60fps GPU-Wave Ocean: PlaneGeometry(2100, 2100, 96, 96) rotated -Math.PI / 2, baked vertex colors (0x00c4d4 turquoise -> 0x0072bc cerulean), flatShading: true, roughness: 0.22, metalness: 0.12, transparent: true, opacity: 0.94. Displace transformed.y via mat.onBeforeCompile + uTime: sin(x * 0.065 + uTime * 1.65) * 0.22 + cos(z * 0.055 + uTime * 1.35) * 0.18, mirrored on CPU in OceanSystem.getWaveHeight(x, z, time). Shoreline foam uses polygonOffset: true, polygonOffsetFactor: -1, polygonOffsetUnits: -1.

4. Performance Budget & 8-Pillar Agentic Longevity Architecture
4.1 Hard Performance Budget (60fps Desktop / Smooth Mobile)
* Draw-Call Ceiling (<= 150 calls/frame): Batch repeated static props (palms, rocks, ferns, grass tufts) into THREE.InstancedMesh (setMatrixAt + setColorAt HSL tinting). Pool all particles (volcano smoke, waterfall mist, splash rings) into pre-allocated InstancedMesh pools with frustumCulled = false.
* GPU-Only Shaders & Zero-GC Frame Loop: Ocean waves, waterfall scrolling, lava glow, and foliage sway run via shader uniforms—never per-frame CPU vertex buffer mutations. Pre-allocate module/class-level Vector3, Quaternion, and Matrix4 scratch objects outside update(dt).
* React Decoupling: Keep requestAnimationFrame inside GameEngine (dt clamped 0.001..0.05), throttle React HUD snapshots to ~9 Hz (110ms) or discrete events, and ban CSS backdrop-blur over the WebGL canvas.
* Distance Throttling (Build 2+) & Telemetry: Pause AnimationMixer and throttle AI beyond ~90m. Expose { calls, triangles, geometries, textures, fps } via window.__SONNY_ISLAND__.getPerfStats().
4.2 Agentic Longevity Architecture (8 Mandatory Pillars)
1. Spatial Contract (src/world/TerrainQuery.ts + src/config/worldAnchors.ts): Pure deterministic getTerrainHeight(x, z), getTerrainNormal(x, z), isWalkable(x, z), getBiomeAt(x, z), a reserved-footprint registry, and 8 named WORLD_ANCHORS (harborSpawn, ranchCenter, jungleTrail, ruinsTerrace, volcanoSummit, lagoonDock, skullCaveMouth, skullCaveInterior).

4. Central InteractionArbitrator (src/engine/InteractionArbitrator.ts): Lightweight IInteractable contract (id, position, radius, priority, icon, canInteract, onInteract) picking one facing-cone winner so Sonny's 3-button layout never fires two actions simultaneously in Build 2+.
